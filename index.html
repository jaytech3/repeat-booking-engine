Repeat Booking Discovery Engine

Version 1. Why do Urban Company customers book again within 90 days, or not? Built on public Google Play reviews of the Urban Company app.

How it works

  1. Collect. The newest public Play Store reviews of the Urban Company app, India, English.
  2. Keep the detailed ones. Reviews under 12 words carry no reason, so they are counted for the star mix but not coded.
  3. Remove what the metric excludes. Reviews about InstaHelp and Native hardware are tagged and dropped, because the 90-day repeat metric covers core services only.
  4. Tag service category and theme. Each review can carry several themes. Rules are transparent and listed in the page source.
  5. Correct for negativity bias. Unhappy customers write long reviews far more often. Each detailed review is reweighted so the star mix matches the star mix of all collected reviews.
  6. Compare. Raw share shows what is loudest. Reweighted share shows what is likely common.

Known limits of version 1

  • Silent non-returners are missing. Customers who were satisfied and simply never came back do not write reviews. A second source is needed for them.
  • Rules, not a language model yet. About half of core reviews match no theme. The next version adds model-based coding and reports agreement against a hand-labelled set.
  • Reweighting only fixes review length bias. People who review at all are still not typical customers.
  • English only, one store, about three months.
